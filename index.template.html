The four rules of the touchline

  • Stamina Matters: Heavy-metal pressing and fast tempo tire your players out, reducing shot composure and tackle speed. Use your 5 bench substitutes wisely!
  • Half-Time Team Talk: At minute 45, address your squad in the locker room. Your words boost stamina, pass accuracy, or defensive grit.
  • Knockout Drama: In the Tournament Cup, drawn matches proceed to 30 minutes of Extra Time and sudden-death penalty shootouts.
  • Deterministic Seeds: Share your match seed with a friend — same seed + same tactics produces the exact same 90-minute outcome every time.
  • Dream Team Matchups: In Dream Team Studio, choose a real club or nation, or let the AI draft an elite opposing XI for you.
